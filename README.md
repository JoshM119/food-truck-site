# food-truck-site

## What changes at each breakpoint

- Phones: Menu cards stacked in 1 column, visit us boxes also stacked in 1 column, heading is 2.5 rem
- From md (768px): menu cards go to 2 columns, visit us boxes are side by side, heading is 4.5rem, and lead is 1.5rem, more padding around cards.
- From lg (992px): cards go to 3 columns, more space on sides of page
